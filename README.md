## 🏛️ Research Profile

**Graduate Student** | Graduate School of Software Convergence, Kookmin University
**Core Focus:** Bridging Physical Systems and Artificial Intelligence via Edge-Native Transformers

### 🔬 Research Interests

* **Maritime DX & Physical AI:** Engineering autonomous maritime systems by integrating Physical AI to digitize complex marine environments and optimize vessel trajectory prediction.
* **Spatio-Temporal Transformer Architectures:** Advancing Transformer-based models to capture long-range dependencies in dynamic physical data, specifically for real-time sensor fusion.
* **Intelligence at the Edge (Edge AI):** Optimizing heavy deep learning architectures for resource-constrained Edge devices to enable low-latency inference in maritime and communication networks.
* **Next-Gen Network AI Systems:** Designing self-optimizing network protocols and AI-driven resource allocation for robust communication in unpredictable physical environments.
* **Hardware-Aware Physical AI:** Developing cross-layer solutions that consider physical constraints (signal interference, power consumption) within the neural architecture search (NAS) process.
